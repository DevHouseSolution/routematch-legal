Privacy Policy

Applies to the Routematch app (customers, package com.techexpress.techexpress_customer) and the Routematch Partner app (vehicle owners, logistics owners and drivers, package com.techexpress.techexpress_driver). Last updated: 24 November 2026.

Routematch Logistics ("Routematch", "we", "us") runs a marketplace that connects people who need goods transported with truck and vehicle owners who have free capacity on a route. This policy explains what information the apps collect, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: mobile number (used for login by one-time password), name, and optionally email address and profile photo. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email and Google account ID.
  • Business details (Partner app): company name, GSTIN, address, vehicle details (number, type, capacity), driver names and mobile numbers.
  • Verification documents (Partner app): photos or scans of driving licence, vehicle RC, insurance, permits and identity documents that you upload for verification.
  • Bank details (Partner app): account holder name, account number and IFSC, used only to pay out your earnings.
  • Booking details: pickup, stops and drop addresses, goods type, weight, photos of goods, receiver name and phone, trip status, invoices and lorry receipts (LR).
  • Location: see section 3.
  • Messages and voice notes: chat messages and voice messages exchanged between a customer and a driver about a booking.
  • Payments: payment amount, status and transaction reference. Card, UPI and net-banking details are entered on Razorpay's secure page and are not stored by us.
  • Ratings and reviews you give or receive.
  • Device information: push-notification token, app version, device model and language, and crash/diagnostic information.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and verify your phone number.
  • To match bookings with vehicles, calculate fares, and run trips (pickup and delivery OTPs, invoices, LR).
  • To verify vehicle owners and drivers before they can take bookings.
  • To process payments, commissions, refunds and payouts.
  • To let customers and drivers chat, call each other and track the trip, and to translate chat messages into the reader's language.
  • To send booking updates and service announcements by push notification.
  • To provide support, prevent fraud and misuse, and meet legal and tax obligations.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show third-party advertising in the apps.

3. Location data

Routematch (customer app) uses your location, only while you are using the app, to fill in pickup and drop points and to show nearby vehicles.

Routematch Partner (driver app) collects precise location to find bookings on your route and, from the moment you accept a trip until it is delivered, even when the app is closed or not in use, to share the vehicle's live position with the customer and with our support team. During this time a persistent "trip in progress" notification is shown. Location tracking stops automatically when the trip is completed or cancelled.

4. Microphone and camera

The microphone is used only when you record a voice message in chat. The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to upload a profile photo, a goods photo or a verification document.

5. Sharing of information

  • With the other party of a booking: after a driver accepts a booking, the customer sees the driver's name, phone number, vehicle number and live location, and the driver sees the customer's and receiver's name, phone number and addresses.
  • Service providers that process data for us: Google Firebase (login, push notifications), Google Maps (maps, routes, addresses), Google Cloud Translation (chat translation), Razorpay (payments) and our cloud hosting provider in India.
  • Authorities when required by law, or to protect the safety of users and the public.

6. Data security and retention

Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections and stored on secured servers with access limited to authorised staff. We keep account data while your account is active. Booking, invoice and payment records are kept for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires (generally 8 years), even after an account is deleted. Other data is deleted or anonymised within 30 days of account deletion.

7. Your rights and choices

  • You can view and edit your profile details in the app.
  • You can turn off location, microphone, camera and notification permissions in your phone's settings (some features will stop working).
  • You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it.
  • You can delete your account at any time: see how to delete your account.

8. Children

The apps are meant for adults running or using a transport business. They are not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. If the changes are significant we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
