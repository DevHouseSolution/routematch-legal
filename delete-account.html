Delete your Routematch account

For the Routematch app and the Routematch Partner app by Routematch Logistics.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to techexpresslogistics.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. Write the mobile number registered on your account and which app you use (Routematch or Routematch Partner).
  3. We will confirm the request with you on that number and delete the account within 30 days.

Before deleting, please finish or cancel any active booking and, for partners, withdraw any wallet balance.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, profile photo and saved locations.
  • Verification documents, vehicle details and bank details (Partner app).
  • Chat messages, voice messages, location history and notification tokens.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Booking, invoice, lorry receipt (LR), payment and payout records are kept for up to 8 years because Indian tax and accounting law requires it. They are not used for any other purpose.
  • Records needed to resolve an open dispute or prevent fraud are kept until the matter is closed.
